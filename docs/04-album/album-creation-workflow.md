# Album Creation Workflow

## Confirmed

At journey completion, say: “Let’s start building your Storied Album.” Do not claim a perfect Album already exists.

The system creates a rough first draft. The traveler then refines:

- photos and captions;
- memories and story order;
- maps and page choices;
- cover and meaningful moments.

AI acts as editorial and design assistant; the traveler remains the author. Publication occurs only after traveler review and choice.
