# Living Album

## Confirmed

A physical Storied Album should be beautiful and complete as a standalone coffee-table book, while selected pages can connect to exact digital memories.

Linked media may include:

- a short video or audio recording;
- a 360-degree image;
- a Campfire reflection;
- a GPS journey replay;
- other meaningful digital media.

Initial implementation may use QR codes. Long term, the marker should feel like Storied Journey design—potentially a compass or another branded treatment—rather than an ugly generic QR code. Scanning opens the media associated with that exact Story Moment, not an entire camera roll.

An important stop may combine photographs, people, Story Moments, Campfire reflections, captions, reviews or experiences, short videos, audio, and other meaningful media. A Living Album marker may connect a printed photograph or Story Moment to its exact associated video, audio, or other digital memory while preserving the underlying journey/story source relationship.

> A Storied Album should be beautiful on a coffee table and magical in your hands.

Hosting duration, privacy, authorization, link durability, offline fallback, and marker design are **TBD**.
