# Sample Album

## Confirmed

At first application launch, show a beautiful cached sample Storied Album using a demonstration journey such as Joyce Journey. The sample establishes both what the publishing product can become and its cost/value proposition, so there are no surprises later in the journey. It is not generated from the new user’s trip. Final pricing remains TBD and any current pricing assumptions are exploratory.

The sample should work offline once included with the application experience. Exact content, disclosure, price presentation, format, and update strategy are **TBD**.

## Acceptance intent

A new traveler can distinguish the sample from personal content and understand that their own journey may later be developed collaboratively into a paid integrated Album.
