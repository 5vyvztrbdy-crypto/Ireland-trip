# Roadmap

## Confirmed strategy

Build backward from the Storied Album. Prioritize the core journey-to-publishing loop and working capabilities over unrelated features.

## Planned capability sequence

1. Canonical Joyce Journey and reservation foundation with offline access and export.
2. Reservation source ingestion, structured extraction, and traveler confirmation.
3. Journey Perspective, conflict awareness, and explainable opportunities.
4. Story Moments, Campfire, prompts, and bulk photo/video capture.
5. Cached sample Album and publisher-independent composition model.
6. Rough first draft and traveler editing.
7. Digital publishing, physical partner integration, and Living Album links.
8. Joyce Journey variants, economics validation, deployment, and four-week marketing readiness.

This ordering is planned architecture, not a fixed schedule. Sprint scope and dates remain **TBD**.

## Sprint 2 planning — exploration controls

The following product direction is confirmed for Sprint 2 planning; implementation scope and final traveler-facing wording remain to be selected:

- retire “Surprise” as the primary exploration concept;
- define a provisionally named “Explore Nearby” or “What's Nearby?” action in Today / Journey Perspective;
- treat exploration as journey-aware recommendation work that can eventually use location, destination, route, reservation anchors, time, preferences, detour tolerance, and Opportunity Meter context;
- keep Unexpected Joy as a discovery the traveler preserves after experiencing it, with paths into Story Moments, Campfire, media/story material, and the Storied Album;
- preserve Pause while evaluating “Take Your Time,” and define it as positively framed replanning of flexible parts of the day;
- protect reservation anchors and avoid prominent placement of exploration controls on the Reservations screen;
- select and validate final user-facing wording before production UI implementation.

This planning update does not authorize production UI changes.

## Sprint 2 planning — Journey Perspective entry point

The following product direction is confirmed for Sprint 2 planning; implementation scope and interface design remain to be selected:

- make “Start Today’s Adventure” the transition from planning mode into active travel mode and the primary entry point to Journey Perspective;
- treat Journey Perspective as the main current-day, in-journey experience rather than another itinerary list or a route to the Reservations screen;
- establish orientation with a map before dense chronology or text;
- communicate current position, next destination, protected reservation anchors, flexible or optional stops, geographic route relationships, and opportunities around the route and schedule;
- consume trusted reservation anchors while showing how flexible parts of the day fit around them;
- plan Journey Perspective as the natural home for exploration, intentional slowdown, Opportunity Meter context, Unexpected Joy recommendations, the next protected reservation, and route-aware timing and detour context;
- leave visual design, layout, map provider, zoom behavior, and interaction model open for later selection and validation.

This planning update does not authorize production UI changes.

## Future publishing exploration — souvenir calendar

A souvenir calendar is an exploratory future publishing concept, not a committed product or Sprint 2 requirement. It could reuse the journey/story/media system to create another physical product from an existing journey. Its inclusion here reinforces that core source objects and the reusable daily Journey Perspective should remain independent of Album pages so different publishing products can compose them differently.

## Future pre-journey capability — Prepare Your Story

Pre-journey story preparation is confirmed product direction but is not authorized for production UI implementation by this documentation update. Future planning should cover:

- concise intentional-capture guidance for photographs, people, short personal video, sound, transitions, and meaningful details;
- personalized near-departure Story Prep informed by journey context;
- selective practical readiness and respectful cultural guidance relevant to the actual destination and itinerary;
- a direct connection to Story Prompts, the Story Engine, Living Album media, and eventual Album quality without upsell framing;
- validation of the final name, interface form, timing, recommendation logic, accessibility, and traveler controls.

The capability must remain focused by asking whether each item will meaningfully improve the journey, prevent a problem, or help preserve the story. It must not expand into a comprehensive destination encyclopedia.
