# Capability Definition of Done

## Confirmed rule

A feature is not delivered merely because a button or visual shell exists. Sprint progress is measured by usable capabilities.

## Working-capability evidence

A capability is ready to present as working when, in proportion to its risk:

- its primary user outcome functions end to end;
- canonical data is persisted and retrieved correctly;
- offline behavior works where the product requires it;
- critical confirmations and failure states are handled;
- source ownership, export, and AI guardrails are respected;
- relevant automated or repeatable acceptance checks pass;
- deployment status is known;
- the Sprint Review includes a real demonstration or evidence.

Exact engineering quality gates are planned and will evolve; they do not reduce the confirmed working-capability standard.
