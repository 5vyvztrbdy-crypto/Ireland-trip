# Sprint Process

## Confirmed

Daily Sprint Reviews occur during active development. Development and deployment are both part of the review. The founder should not have to search through a prototype to discover changes.

Each short presentation shows:

- capabilities developed and actually working;
- screenshots or demonstrations;
- deployment and hosting progress;
- Storied Album progress;
- important business and publishing developments;
- blockers;
- next sprint priorities.

Visual-only or nonfunctional work must be labeled accurately rather than presented as delivered capability.
