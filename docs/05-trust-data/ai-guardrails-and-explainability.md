# AI Guardrails and Explainability

## Confirmed

AI may suggest, organize, inspire, extract, and create drafts. It must not:

- silently change important itinerary decisions;
- overwrite source memories;
- present invented trip facts as truth;
- replace the traveler as final decision maker and author.

Meaningful recommendations should explain why the traveler is seeing them. Travel-critical extraction requires confirmation before authority. AI-generated Album language remains editable.

## Planned architecture

AI outputs should retain provenance, confidence or uncertainty where useful, source links, confirmation state, and revision history. Exact models, providers, evaluation criteria, and explanation interface are **TBD**.
