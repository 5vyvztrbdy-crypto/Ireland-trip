# Joyce Journey Showcase Plan

## Confirmed

Joyce Journey is the first major Storied Album showcase and commercial proof of concept. Marketing should lead with the finished Album and emotional result rather than screenshots of a travel-planning application.

The showcase should demonstrate the full lifecycle: trusted planning context, lived travel, intentional media, Story Moments, Campfire reflections, Unexpected Joys, rough draft, traveler editing, final publishing, and Living Album media.
