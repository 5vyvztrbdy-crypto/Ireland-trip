# Joyce Journey Acceptance Dataset

## Confirmed

The founder’s Iceland/Ireland trip is the primary acceptance dataset and first real-world product test. The complete trip must remain represented. Missing reservations or destinations are product failures, not cosmetic issues.

Joyce Journey also becomes the first major Storied Album showcase and commercial proof of concept.

## Current implementation evidence

The prototype contains seven dated journey days from August 29 through September 4, 2026, with Iceland and Ireland stops, bases, food suggestions, flights, rental car, selected lodging, a dinner reservation, and heritage activities. These entries are observed prototype content and are not automatically confirmed travel facts.

The prototype explicitly warns that a 10:00 AM Dublin rental-car return conflicts with a 10:40 AM transatlantic departure. That warning is evidence of a potential critical conflict, not a substitute for traveler-confirmed reservation sources.

## Dataset requirement

An authoritative dataset must be built from retained source confirmations and traveler review. Until then, completeness cannot be claimed.
