# Album Variant Experiments

## Confirmed

After the Iceland/Ireland trip, approximately three or four physical Storied Album variants should be created from the same real journey to determine the strongest commercial publishing model.

Possible experimental directions named in the brief are:

- classic/story-first;
- adventure/map-forward;
- heritage/reflection-forward;
- premium coffee-table treatment.

These are experiment concepts, not confirmed permanent product tiers.

Evaluation criteria, publishers, materials, page counts, costs, research participants, purchase-intent method, and selection thresholds must be defined before testing.
