# Joyce Journey Completeness Checklist

## Acceptance requirements

- Every journey day, destination, route segment, and overnight base is represented.
- Every reservation has a canonical record, retained source where available, and confirmation state.
- Flights, rental car, lodging, timed attractions, and dining reservations appear consistently across all projections.
- Timing and geographic conflicts are surfaced with explainable before/after impact.
- Required journey information is usable offline.
- Captured photos, videos, Story Moments, Campfire entries, reviews, and Unexpected Joys remain associated with the correct context.
- User-owned source material can be exported without Album purchase.
- The rough Album draft traces content to sources and remains traveler-editable.
- Living Album markers resolve to the exact approved digital memory.

## TBD inputs

The complete reservation inventory, original confirmations, exact addresses, final schedule, and confirmed destination list must be supplied or traveler-verified. Prototype content alone cannot close this checklist.
