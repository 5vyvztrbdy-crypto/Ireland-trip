# Product Requirements

## Confirmed outcomes

- A journey remains useful offline for itinerary, reservations, essential information, and memory capture.
- One canonical reservation supplies all downstream experiences.
- Critical extracted reservation facts become authoritative only after traveler confirmation.
- Travelers can export their underlying source material without buying an Album.
- Recommendations expose meaningful reasons.
- The system accepts large, imperfectly organized media collections.
- A rough Album draft can be edited by the traveler before publishing.
- Selected printed pages can open their exact linked digital memory.
- Joyce Journey is represented completely enough to serve as the primary acceptance dataset and first commercial showcase.

## Quality rule

A requirement is not delivered when only its button, screen, or sample state exists. Delivery requires functioning behavior, appropriate persistence, failure handling, and demonstrable acceptance evidence.
