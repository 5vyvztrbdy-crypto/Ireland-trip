# Scope and Non-Goals

## Confirmed scope

- Journey planning and trusted reservations.
- Geographic and temporal Journey Perspective.
- Offline travel utility.
- Intentional memory capture and story development.
- Integrated digital and physical publishing.
- Exact links from selected physical pages to Living Album media.

## Confirmed near-term non-goals

Do not build unnecessary social networking, gamification, or unrelated features before the core loop works. Storied Journey should initially own the creative/storytelling layer rather than operate its own printing company.
