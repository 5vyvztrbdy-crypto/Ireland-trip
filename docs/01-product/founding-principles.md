# Founding Principles

## Confirmed

1. **Offline First.** Core itinerary, reservations, memory capture, and essential trip information remain useful without signal.
2. **Your Memories Belong to You.** User-created source material is exportable without an Album purchase.
3. **AI Is Your Guide, Never Your Boss.** AI may suggest, organize, inspire, and draft, but cannot silently change important itinerary decisions, overwrite memories, or assert invented trip facts. The traveler decides and authors.
4. **No Surprise Rule.** Meaningful recommendations should explain why they appear.
5. **Build Working Capabilities, Not Demonstrations.** A visual shell is not delivered functionality.
6. **Monetize Publishing, Not Planning.** Core planning and travel use should trend toward free; revenue primarily comes from integrated digital and physical publishing.

These principles are product constraints and should become acceptance tests where practicable.
