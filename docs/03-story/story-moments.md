# Story Moments

## Confirmed

A Story Moment is a meaningful moment worth preserving, not merely a photograph. It may connect experience, place, people, time, media, reflection, and later Album treatment.

A Story Moment must preserve traveler authorship. AI may suggest grouping or wording, but it cannot silently overwrite the underlying memory or declare an unconfirmed event to have occurred.

## Planned architecture

A Story Moment would have stable identity and optional links to itinerary events, reservations, media, Campfire entries, GPS context, reviews, and Album placements. Detailed fields and lifecycle are **TBD**.
