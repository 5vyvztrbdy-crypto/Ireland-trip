# Campfire

## Confirmed

Campfire is a reflective memory-capture experience for feelings, observations, context, stories, and details that photographs alone cannot preserve.

Campfire entries are traveler-owned source material and must be exportable without an Album purchase. AI may help organize or edit copies for an Album, but the source entry remains intact unless the traveler changes it.

## Current implementation

The prototype’s journal text approximates a small part of Campfire but does not name or model it as a distinct experience.
