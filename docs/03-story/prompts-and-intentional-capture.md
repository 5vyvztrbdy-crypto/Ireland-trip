# Prompts and Intentional Capture

## Confirmed

Story Prompts gently encourage media and reflection that will matter later. The goal is better intentional media, not indiscriminately more media.

Confirmed examples include taking one photo together, recording ten seconds of motion, capturing sound or feeling, photographing morning coffee or a first bite, and recording a brief moment before leaving.
