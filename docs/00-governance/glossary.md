# Glossary

- **Campfire** — reflective capture of feelings, observations, context, stories, and details that media alone may miss.
- **Canonical reservation** — the trusted structured record that supplies all reservation views and downstream uses.
- **Journey Perspective** — geographic and temporal understanding of a journey beyond turn-by-turn navigation.
- **Living Album** — a physical Storied Album whose selected pages connect to exact digital memories while remaining complete as a standalone book.
- **Opportunity Meter** — positive framing that shows where itinerary flexibility creates opportunity.
- **Reservation source** — an original screenshot, image, document, or later email used to support extracted reservation data.
- **Story Moment** — a meaningful experience worth preserving, whether or not it has a photograph.
- **Story Prompt** — a gentle suggestion for intentional capture of media or reflection.
- **Storied Album** — the paid, integrated publishing product made from journey source material; it is not merely a photo book or PDF.
- **Unexpected Joy** — a prompted or unprompted discovery that becomes meaningful during the journey.
