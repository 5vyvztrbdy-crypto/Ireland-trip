# Pricing Assumptions

## Exploratory assumptions — not final

- Approximately $5 has been discussed for a low-friction digital integrated Album.
- Earlier illustrations included approximately $1 AI cost per Album.
- Earlier illustrations included approximately $20 publishing and shipping cost.
- Physical Album concepts around $28 have been discussed.

These numbers are scenario inputs, not approved pricing, verified costs, or forecasts. They require validation against actual publishing partners, shipping, payment fees, Storied Journey costs, and target margin.

No implementation should hard-code them as product truth.
