# Go-to-Market

## Confirmed

Commercial beta should test willingness to buy with strangers rather than rely primarily on friends and family. Joyce Journey is the first showcase.

After the Iceland/Ireland trip, create approximately three or four physical Album variants from the same real journey. Possible experimental directions are classic/story-first, adventure/map-forward, heritage/reflection-forward, and premium coffee-table treatment. These are prototypes for choosing a commercial format, not confirmed permanent tiers.

The target is readiness for a real Facebook/Instagram marketing campaign approximately four weeks after returning from Ireland. Marketing should lead with the finished Album and emotional result, enabling a prospective customer to think, “I want that for my next trip.” The Album should become a word-of-mouth object.

Campaign budget, audience, channels beyond those named, creative, landing experience, and success thresholds are **TBD**.
