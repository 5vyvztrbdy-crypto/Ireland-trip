# Business Simulator Specification

## Confirmed future capability

The simulator should model active users, completed journeys, trips per family per year, digital/standard/premium conversion, AI cost, manufacturing, shipping, payment fees, acquisition cost, gross revenue, gross margin, contribution margin, and break-even.

## Exploratory scenario assumptions

- 25% digital story purchase;
- 15% physical Album purchase;
- 10% premium Album purchase.

These are not validated forecasts. The simulator must label assumptions, support scenario ranges, and keep observed data separate. App Store rating counts must never be represented as actual user counts.

Definitions, cohort treatment, returns, taxes, repeat purchases, and reporting implementation are **TBD**.
