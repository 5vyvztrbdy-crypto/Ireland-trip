# Publishing Partner Strategy

## Confirmed

Storied Journey should initially own the creative and storytelling layer while a publishing partner handles manufacturing and fulfillment. It should not initially become a printing company and should avoid hard-coding to one publisher.

Snapfish and other high-quality photo-book producers may be evaluated; naming a candidate is not a partner selection.

Partner criteria:

- print quality and competitive delivered cost;
- wholesale or partner economics;
- API or automation capability;
- reliable fulfillment and international shipping;
- multiple formats and premium options;
- reordering;
- support for Storied Journey layouts and Living Album markers.
