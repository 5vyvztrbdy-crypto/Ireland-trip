# Business Model

## Confirmed

Core travel planning and travel use should trend toward free. Travelers are not charged to retrieve their own source material. Revenue primarily occurs when Storied Journey transforms that material into an integrated digital or physical publishing product.

A premium physical edition should exist. Early margins should encourage adoption and user-base growth rather than maximize first-purchase profit. Repeat family use—potentially two or three meaningful journeys per year—is strategically important. “Albums per Family per Year” is a useful future metric.
