# Opportunity Meter and Unexpected Joys

## Confirmed

The Opportunity Meter uses positive framing to help travelers understand where flexibility creates opportunity. Unexpected Joys include prompted or unprompted worthwhile places, overlooks, short sightseeing stops, and other discoveries near the route.

Recommendations must follow the No Surprise Rule: explain why the opportunity is shown. Relevant factors may include route relationship, available time, reservation anchors, and traveler context, but the exact model is **TBD**.

## Current implementation

“Surprise Me” randomly chooses a generic category and opens a Google Maps search. It explicitly states that it does not search live businesses. This is a demonstration, not the confirmed capability.
